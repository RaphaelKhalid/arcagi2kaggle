01 / Executive read

A strong theory campaign.
Not yet a score claim.

The loop produced a coherent, release-gated path toward the current low-70s output frontier. It also killed many attractive ideas before they could poison a submission: hard palette masks, fixed DSL optimism, value-blind scheduling, and raw correlated voting.

The notebook itself remains the control. The new ideas are deliberately shadow lanes until they beat it on frozen candidates, unique classes, wall-clock, and pass@2.

02 / Overnight findings

What survived 217 iterations

? Hover or focus dotted terms for definitions.

01

The metric changes the architecture

ARC rewards output positions, so the right objective is expected unique correct classes per second. A task-level average can hide wasted compute on already-solved positions and correlated replicas.

gain / second = unresolved mass × novelty × selector recovery ÷ cost
02

Exact fit is not proof

A program that reproduces every demonstration belongs in the version space; it is not automatically the target rule. Promotion needs typed effects, counterexamples, complexity control, and task-disjoint calibration.

03

Use specialists, keep the baseline

Object graphs, role/effect equations, cellular rules, scene graphs, motif panels, and evolutionary search recovered narrow proposal families. None earned the right to replace the neural baseline. Their value is complementary recall.

04

Correlation is an accounting problem

Augmentation views and replicas are not independent votes by default. Preserve provenance, collapse duplicate semantic outputs, and credit independent lineages separately.

05

Shape is the safe structural gate

The paranoid size predictor fired correctly on 882/882 training cases and 109/109 evaluation cases. The allowlisted shape family reached 727/727 and 119/119. Extra fired-count was not enough to justify promotion because aggregate runtime savings were small.

06

Palette hard masks are unsafe

Exact palette rules had cross-fold violations. The correct design is full palette support with soft evidence or mixture branches. A hard constraint that is usually right but occasionally wrong destroys recall exactly where the search is already fragile.

07

Long grids break absolute thresholds

A fixed sequence threshold of −log(0.2) demands mean token probabilities near 0.996 at length 400 and 0.998 at length 900. Use an incumbent-preserving union of absolute and calibrated length-normalized frontiers, with a conformal gate.

08

The controller is the learned component

Four L4s and 12 hours make allocation a first-class model. Route work by marginal output value divided by calibrated cost, maintain a deadline reserve, and spend more on unresolved and complementary positions.

09

Cost calibration is the clearest operational win

Training-only area/demo buckets reduced evaluation task-cost MAE from 625.43 to 81.61 and raised Pearson correlation from 0.9916 to 0.9977. In a 240-task geometry replay, calibrated ordering completed 192/207/219/229/237 tasks versus 179/193/206/217/226 for raw visible-cost ordering at 55–95% capacity.

10

Two finals are a portfolio, not a second thought

Each final must be a complete valid submission. Optimize complementary private-half risk at the whole-submission level; never assemble a malformed “best per position” hybrid after the fact.

y⋆ = arg maxy ∈ C(D)   [   log P(y | D) + λ·proof(y) − μ·correlation(y) − ν·shift-risk(y)   ]

The candidate set stays open whenever a constraint is uncertain. This is the monotonicity invariant: adding a lane may add a candidate, but must not delete the incumbent merely because the new lane abstains.
